Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

gmail-yun is a personal, single-user application. It is not distributed to or used by anyone other than its owner.

Data accessed

With the owner's consent, the app uses the Google OAuth scopes https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.compose (create and manage drafts) and https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly (read messages and drafts) on the owner's own Gmail account.

How the data is used

Gmail data is used only to create, list and delete draft emails and to read message information at the owner's request. It is not used for advertising, analytics, or any other purpose.

Storage and sharing

The app stores only an OAuth token on the owner's own computer so that it can keep working without asking for sign-in again. Email content is not copied to any external server, is not sold, and is not shared with third parties. The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
